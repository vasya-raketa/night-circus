ART BIBLE — "Night Circus" (2D mobile arcade game)
Style: flat 2D vector cartoon, chunky rounded shapes, thick dark outline (#1A1030) on gameplay objects, no outline on backgrounds. Premium mobile-game look, magical and warm, slightly Pixar-like charm.
Palette: royal blue #1E2A6B and purple #3B1F6B (tent interior), crimson #C8283E and cream #F5E8CC (tent stripes), gold #F5B93D (anything the player can grab), teal #2EC4B6 (player costume accents, perfect-catch cues), danger red-orange #FF4D2E (hazards only), warm spotlight #FFF4D6.
Rules: grabbable = gold, dangerous = red-orange, background never uses full-strength gold or red. Must read clearly on a 360x640 phone screen.
Lighting: soft warm spotlights from above, gentle rim light on characters and props.
Original designs only: no logos, no text, no clowns, nothing resembling existing games or films.
